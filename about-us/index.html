Topten (Owerri-North) Multipurpose Cooperative Society Limited

Topten (Owerri-North) Multipurpose Cooperative Society Limited is a cooperative established in 2017 and registered in Imo State, Nigeria. The cooperative focuses on real estate marketing and trading, thrift contributions, food distribution and related services.
