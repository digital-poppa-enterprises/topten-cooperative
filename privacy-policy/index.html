Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Topten Co-Operative is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of the personal and financial information entrusted to us by our members, subscribers, property buyers, and website visitors.

1. Information We Collect

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal data when you visit our website: www.toptencoop.com.ng , apply for cooperative membership, request micro-credit facilities, or engage in real estate trading transactions with us.

2. Lawful Data Processing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. However, we may share your data with trusted third parties when necessary to deliver our services.
