Please read these terms carefully. These eleven binding clauses govern subscriber participation, thrift contribution schedules, administrative charges, and cooperative obligations.

Official Policy Instrument • Operational Cycle 2026/2027 Administered by TOPTEN Multipurpose Cooperative Society Limited
1.

No withdrawal or refund of contribution before the end of the thrift period.

2.

If contribution is not completed, the Admin Charge is deducted in full and the balance is paid within 30 days after the end of the thrift period.

3.

Payment to subscribers who complete contribution according to plan is made within 4 working days of the end of the thrift period.

4.

Payment to subscribers who default is made within 30 days of the end of the thrift period.

5.

Subscribers can only receive payment directly into their own bank account except where otherwise approved.

6.

Allocation for land/home is based on the first subscriber to complete payment according to the plan/period.

7.

Land/home subscribers who fail to complete payment at the end of the thrift period receive 60% of the total contributed within 60 days; 40% is deducted as default/Admin Charge.

8.

₦1,000 is charged for a copy of the collection booklet. Keep it safe for updating payments and records.

9.

Alteration or loss makes the booklet invalid and attracts a ₦1,000 replacement charge.

10.

TOPTEN is not liable for payments not made to its bank account.

11.

Agents/members are not authorised to receive money on behalf of TOPTEN.

Ratified and in effect for all active cooperative memberships.
